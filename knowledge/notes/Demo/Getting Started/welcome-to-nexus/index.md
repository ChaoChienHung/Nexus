# Welcome to Nexus (Demo)

This sample note demonstrates how **Nexus** turns plain-text Markdown and JSON files into an interactive [[concept.knowledge_base]].

## Try These Features Right Here

1. **Sentence Comments**: Highlight any sentence in this article to attach a comment saved to `comments.json`, or click the highlighted sentence above / the comment card on the right to jump between them.
2. **Inline Terminology**: Click [[concept.evergreen_note]] or [[concept.ontology]] to open its definition, relations, and BM25 Top 3 related documents in the right sidebar.
3. **Direct Editing & Marginalia**: Click **Edit** in the top bar to edit `index.md` directly (`⌘S` saves to disk), or open the **備註 / Notes** tab to edit only the `## 備註` section below.
4. **Global Search & Settings**: Press `⌘K` to search across all notes and terms, or click the gear icon in the sidebar to switch between **繁體中文 / English** and **Light / Dark** mode.
